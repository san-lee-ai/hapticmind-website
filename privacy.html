Privacy Policy

How We Handle Your Data

HapticMind is built to keep your information on your own devices. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. The app doesn't send your information to us or to anyone else, and we never sell it.

Last updated November 5, 2026.

Information Stored on Your Devices

HapticMind saves your settings and a history of your sessions: when each one happened, how long it lasted and which breathing pattern you used. This stays on your iPhone, and it's included in your device backups if you use them. Your iPhone and Apple Watch pass settings and sessions directly between each other, so the two stay in step.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional, and sessions work fully without it. If you allow access, HapticMind reads your heart rate to compare it before and during a session. That comparison is worked out on your iPhone and isn't saved. Apple Watch also uses your heart rate during a session, and saves sessions of a minute or longer to Apple Health as Mind and Body workouts. Your Health data is never used for advertising and is never shared. You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple, and we never see your payment details. The app asks the App Store what you've bought so it can unlock Plus.

Sharing With Others

We don't share your information with anyone. HapticMind contains no third-party analytics or advertising services.

Keeping or Deleting Your Data

Your data stays on your devices until you delete it. Deleting HapticMind from your iPhone and Apple Watch removes its settings and session history. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

This Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its icons from Cloudflare. Like most web services, they may record technical details such as your IP address when you visit. We don't use cookies or analytics here.

Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy periodically. We will notify you of changes by posting the updated policy on this page. Continued use of HapticMind constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
