One tap to begin

Home shows your week at a glance, and starts a session with your usual choices. Or start from how you'd like to feel with Quick Breathe: Relax, Focus, Sleep or Reset. Whether you're sighted, blind or simply looking for some quiet, HapticMind keeps things simple.

Breathe by touch

  • Box and 4-7-8 breathing, with a different feel for each phase
  • Soft, Medium or Strong taps
  • 5, 10 and 15 minute sessions
  • Learn Haptics lets you try two breaths of any pattern before you begin
  • Taps ease off gently as a session ends

A calm session screen

A quiet countdown, then a dark screen where the circle breathes inside a ring that fills as the session passes. Pause, resume or end any time, and say how you feel afterwards with one optional tap.

Made for Apple Watch

  • Run a session on your wrist, by itself or alongside your iPhone
  • Taps keep going with your wrist down
  • A session on both devices only counts once
  • A complication to open HapticMind from your watch face

See your progress

Your week at a glance, total sessions and your streak, for everyone. If you bought an earlier version, your settings and progress carry over.

  • A gentle reminder at a moment that suits your day, skipped if you've already meditated
  • Widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen
  • Ask Siri to start a session
  • Save sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes
